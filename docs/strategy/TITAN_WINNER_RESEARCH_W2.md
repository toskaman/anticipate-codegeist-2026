# TITAN winner research — wave 2 (12 Nov 2026)

Purpose: close the coverage gap left by wave 1 (17 projects) to an honest
20+ projects, and add a non-Devpost competition (NASA Space Apps).

Method: primary sources only (official winner announcements + official rules
pages). Devpost submission bodies remain 403 to unauthenticated fetches, so
per-project detail rests on announcement text; placements below are quoted
exactly as the official source states them.

## W2-1 — Compliant Workflows for Jira (Codegeist 2025, category winner)

- Competition: Codegeist 2025: Atlassian Williams Racing Edition (308
  submitted projects, 21 winners, $120k pool; winners announced ~Feb 2026).
- Placement: official Atlassian winners-blog category winner; secondary
  coverage (sanovibe) reports 3rd place in its category — placement
  ordinal NOT independently confirmed, do not cite beyond "category winner".
- Problem/user: regulated teams (pharma, medtech, fintech) must prove
  every Jira workflow meets spec without slowing delivery.
- Transformation: guardrails + governance embedded in everyday Jira
  workflows ("every lap meets spec").
- Transferable: CONFIRMS wave-1 lesson 3 (governance as the feature).
  Closest Codegeist precedent to ANTICIPATE's firewall framing.
  Differentiation: they configure compliance; ANTICIPATE simulates
  counterfactuals, enforces at the transition, and verifies execution
  by Jira read-back.

## W2-2 — Sidekick4All (Codegeist 2025, 2nd Business Teams + Best Rovo Apps)

- Official Atlassian winners blog confirms both the category podium and
  the Rovo bonus on the same entry.
- Problem/user: cross-functional teams cannot see how Jira work lines up
  with calendars (standups, releases, milestones).
- Transformation: bidirectional Jira↔calendar sync + capacity view.
- Transferable: another multi-scoreboard precedent (category + Rovo
  bonus on one entry) supporting our optional Best-Agents second bonus
  (`docs/submission/BONUS_AGENTS_DRAFT.md`). Mechanism: cross-tool sync
  that removes coordination toil.

## W2-3 — Resonant Exoplanets (NASA Space Apps 2025, Best Use of Data)

- Competition: NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2025 (18,860
  teams; 10 global winners announced 18–19 Dec 2025, official NASA).
- Problem/user: astronomers drown in telescope/satellite spectra;
  manual exoplanet + biosignature hunting does not scale.
- Transformation: AI pipeline ingests mission data (Kepler, TESS, JWST,
  Hubble), detects transits, classifies candidates, checks atmospheric
  chemistry, and outputs plots + auto-generated reports.
- Transferable: automate expert analysis AND output the artifact
  (report/verdict), not a chat reply. Direct parallel to our
  verdict + Prevention Passport output (wave-1 lesson 5).
